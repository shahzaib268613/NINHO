# NINHO reference implementation

This revision follows the seven embedded screen references in the supplied NINHO Build Needs PowerPoint:

- Slide 11: login / six-digit email-code demonstration, client account request and professional access request.
- Slide 12: realtor dashboard, my/agency navigation, deadlines, meeting requests, cases, calendar, listings and agency performance.
- Slide 13: lawyer dashboard, document requests, client cases, tasks, deadline reminders, meeting preparation and saved questions.
- Slide 14: administration overview, agency ranking/drill-down, professional approvals, billing, platform charts, quick stats and fastest responders.
- Slide 15: property page, gallery, school/sports context, map, viewing slots, recommendations, sample cost estimate and guided journey.
- Slide 16: buyer purchase, five-stage progress, participants, documents and per-document visibility, deadlines, translated group chat and manual payment records.
- Slide 17: professional client purchase, team progress/activity, request call, request document, reminders, chat, deadlines and commission.

The burgundy/ivory palette, serif headings, outlined panels, architectural sidebar and compact layout follow the client references. Architectural illustration, property photos, map and sample portraits are cropped directly from the supplied PowerPoint and stored in public/brand. No complete client brief or commercial proposal is published.

All records, charts, document/signature states, reminders and external provider connections remain browser-local demo data. No real payments, emails, legal signatures, certified invoices or production authentication are executed. Admin can configure buyer response-time visibility and working hours. Generated statements are explicitly identified as non-tax demo statements. Production backend and integrations remain outside this interactive frontend demonstration.
